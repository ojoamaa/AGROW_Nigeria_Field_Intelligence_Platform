# AGROW Product Alignment

## Product name
AGROW — Agricultural Geographic Registration & Operations Workspace

## Operating model

### Public verification
- Available from the landing page without administrator access.
- Search by Farmer ID or registered phone number.
- Signed individual Farmer QR codes open authenticated verification records.
- Intended for farmers, distribution teams, field monitors and institutional partners, cooperatives and agribusinesses.

### Field agent workspace
- State/LGA-restricted access.
- Structured farmer enumeration Sections 1–11.
- Geographic coordinates, farmer photo, identity evidence and operational remarks.
- Farmer verification during distribution through the shared verification workspace.

### Institutional administration
- National and state-level monitoring.
- Agent oversight, beneficiary totals, land coverage, NIN status and operational analytics.
- Master registry export and platform-access QR.

## Product principles
1. One farmer, one persistent Farmer ID.
2. Every Farmer ID has a signed verification QR.
3. Public verification exposes farmer verification details, not unrestricted administrative data.
4. Field agents see only their authorised geography and records.
5. Authorised institutional users receive consolidated operational intelligence.
6. Registration, distribution and verification use one database source of truth.
